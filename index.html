      Before you meet someone
      
      
        
        
      • Meet in a busy public place, in daylight if you can.
        • 
        
      • Tell a friend who you're meeting, where and when. The check-in tool writes the message for you.
        • 
        
      • Arrange your own way there and back.
        • 
        
      • Don't share your home address, room number or timetable early on.
        • 
        
      • Never send money, airtime or M-Pesa codes to someone you haven't met, whatever the reason.
        • 
        
      • If something feels wrong, leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
