Safety tools are part of the app

Meeting people is better when you can check, tell a friend and get help fast. These are in TUMingle from the start.

  • Scam check

    Paste a message to look for common warning signs. It's a keyword check, so it can miss scams. Use it as a second opinion.

  • Date check-in

    Write one message for a friend with who you're meeting, where and when. Send it on WhatsApp.

  • Block and report

    Block anyone, or report a profile or a chat. Reports are private.

  • Emergency numbers

    Police 999, emergency 112 and the GBV helpline 1195 sit behind the safety button once you're in.

Date ideas around Mombasa

Places around town, sorted by vibe, each with a Maps link for directions. For a first date, pick somewhere public and tell a friend.

  • Fort Jesus

    Old Town

    A Portuguese-built fort and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Plenty to look at and talk about.

  • Mama Ngina Waterfront

    Mombasa Island

    A seafront park over the Likoni Channel. Sit by the sea and watch the ships come and go.

  • Nyali Beach

    Nyali

    A quieter beach, so it's easier to hear each other talk.
